memelab 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 Last updated: 2026-11-26

memelab 是一個自用的內容帳號工具，只用來在 memelab 自己的社群帳號（Threads、Instagram、YouTube、TikTok）發文、回覆留言、查看自己帳號的數據。它不提供給其他使用者登入或使用。

memelab is a single-account tool used only to publish posts, reply to comments and read statistics on memelab's own social accounts (Threads, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok). It is not offered to other users.

會存什麼 / What we store

  • 我們自己發出的貼文紀錄（內容、時間、貼文編號）。Records of posts we publish (text, time, post ID).
  • 讀到的公開留言，以及我們的回覆紀錄。Public comments on our posts and our replies to them.
  • 帳號的粉絲數與貼文瀏覽數等統計。Follower counts and post view statistics of our own account.

存在哪 / Where it is stored

以上資料只存在我們自己的電腦本機，不上傳到其他伺服器。All of the above is stored only on our own local computer and is not uploaded to any other server.

分享 / Sharing

我們不出售、不出租、也不分享任何資料給第三方。We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

刪除要求 / Deletion requests

如果你希望刪除與你有關的留言紀錄，請寄信到 memelab888tw@gmail.com，我們會在 30 天內刪除並回覆。To request deletion of records related to you, email memelab888tw@gmail.com; we will delete them and reply within 30 days.
