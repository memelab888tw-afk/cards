memelab 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 Last updated: 2026-11-26

memelab 是一個自用的內容帳號工具，只用來在 memelab 自己的社群帳號（Threads、Instagram、YouTube、TikTok）發文、回覆留言、查看自己帳號的數據。它不提供給其他使用者登入或使用。

memelab is a single-account tool used only to publish posts, reply to comments and read statistics on memelab's own social accounts (Threads, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok). It is not offered to other users.

會存什麼 / What we store

  • 我們自己發出的貼文紀錄（內容、時間、貼文編號）。Records of posts we publish (text, time, post ID).
  • 讀到的公開留言，以及我們的回覆紀錄。Public comments on our posts and our replies to them.
  • 帳號的粉絲數與貼文瀏覽數等統計。Follower counts and post view statistics of our own account.

存在哪 / Where it is stored

以上資料只存在我們自己的電腦本機，不上傳到其他伺服器。All of the above is stored only on our own local computer and is not uploaded to any other server.

分享 / Sharing

我們不出售、不出租、也不分享任何資料給第三方。We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

YouTube

本工具使用 YouTube API Services。使用本工具即表示同意 YouTube 服務條款，並適用 Google 隱私權政策。我們從 YouTube 只拿自己頻道的影片清單與統計數字，不存其他使用者的資料。你可以隨時在 Google 帳戶權限頁 撤銷授權。

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. From YouTube we read only our own channel's video list and statistics; we store no other user's data. Access can be revoked at any time at the Google security settings page.

TikTok

本工具用 TikTok Login Kit 登入 memelab 自己的 TikTok 帳號，只讀取帳號暱稱與頭像（user.info.basic），並用 Content Posting API 把我們自己做的影片發到這個帳號。每次發文前，由帳號擁有者自己選誰可以看、留言與合拍設定，按下「發布」才會發出。我們不讀取、不保存其他 TikTok 使用者的資料。可在 TikTok App「設定與隱私 → 安全性 → 管理應用程式權限」撤銷授權。

This tool uses TikTok Login Kit to sign in to memelab's own TikTok account and reads only the account's nickname and avatar (user.info.basic). It uses the Content Posting API to post videos we made to that account. Before every post, the account owner chooses the privacy level and comment/duet/stitch settings and must press Post. We do not read or store data of any other TikTok user. Access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.

刪除要求 / Deletion requests

如果你希望刪除與你有關的留言紀錄，請寄信到 memelab888tw@gmail.com，我們會在 30 天內刪除並回覆。To request deletion of records related to you, email memelab888tw@gmail.com; we will delete them and reply within 30 days.
